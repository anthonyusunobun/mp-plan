About MP Plan

What is MP Plan?
MP Plan connects 2 people:

1. Person with SKILL but no MONEY - e.g. Amina can bake, but has no oven.
2. Person with MONEY but wants to EARN - e.g. You have ₦50,000, you want profit.

You fund them. They work. You BOTH gain.

💚 Contribute - You give, they work, you get back + small profit (e.g. 20%).
📈 Invest - You give bigger, you get back + bigger profit (e.g. 30%).

Our Promise: No scam. Verified owners only. Updates + proof. Report bad people.

❓ Help / FAQ - Most Asked

1. How do I get my money back? ▶

When you Contribute / Invest, you pick a plan. Owner works for X months (e.g. 3 months). After that, owner pays you back + profit. You will see it in Earnings. If owner delays, chat them on WhatsApp, then Contact Us.

2. What is Contribute vs Invest? ▶

Contribute = Small money, small profit (e.g. Give ₦5,000, get ₦6,000 = 20%). Invest = Bigger money, bigger profit (e.g. Give ₦50,000, get ₦65,000 = 30%). Both work same way - they work, you gain.

3. Is MP Plan safe? How do I avoid scam? ▶

Fund only VERIFIED (green ✅) owners with photo + 2+ updates. Start SMALL (₦2,000) first. Chat owner on WhatsApp before you fund big. Report any scam in Safety tab.

4. I forgot my password. What do I do? ▶

On Log In page, tap Forgot Password. Enter your email. It will show your password (demo version). In real version, we email you a reset link.

5. How do I create a plan to get funding? ▶

Go to My Plans > Fill Title, What you will do, Amount Needed, Payback %, Months > Publish. Then post 2 updates with proof to become Verified. Then Share on WhatsApp to get funders.

6. When will I get paid? How does payment work? ▶

Paystack is under review. For now, payment is simulated in app. Once Paystack approves, real card / transfer payment will activate. Payback will be sent to your bank / Paystack.

7. I funded someone and they disappeared. Help! ▶

Go to Safety > Report Scam. Then Contact Us immediately with plan name + amount + proof. We will hide their plan and help you contact them. Always fund small first to avoid big loss.
