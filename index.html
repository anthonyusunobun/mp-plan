About Us

Who Are We?
MP Plan was started in Benin City, Edo State by Anthony Smith.

We saw a big problem: Many talented people in Benin City have skill - baking, farming, tailoring, poultry - but they have NO money to start.

And many people HAVE money but they have NO skill / NO time to work.

So we connected them.
How MP Plan Works - 3 Steps
1. Talent Creates Plan - "I need ₦120,000 for oven. I will pay back 20% in 3 months."
2. Funder Funds It - You give ₦5,000 (Contribute) or ₦50,000 (Invest).
3. Both Gain - Talent works, posts updates with proof, pays you back + profit. Talent grows business, you grow money.
Our Promise
✅ No scam - We show updates + proof
✅ Start small - Test with ₦5,000 first
✅ Verified badge - Only people with 2+ updates get Verified ✅
✅ Report button - Report anyone who scams
Our Vision
To help 10,000 talented people in Edo State and Nigeria start business, without begging, without bank loan.

Our Mission
Make funding simple, safe, and profitable for both sides.
